## Vooby Mods (training input mods)

Small control changes for practice, each a switch under **F1 > Game > Vooby Mods**, in sub menus (All characters, Fox & Falco, Options)
(saved in `port-settings.ini`). Each one adds a real press to your pad before the game reads it,
in the same place as Auto L-cancel, so replays, the input display and the other player in Direct
get it as your input (it cannot desync, and the other players need nothing installed). They follow
your controller, not a player slot, and never touch CPUs. All are off by default. They work
offline, in Direct and in Teams; Ranked, Unranked, Party or a vanilla-only session turns them off
whatever is saved.

| Mod | ini key | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-stick up on the ground: jump-cancelled up-smash | `padmod_cstick_up_jump` | A grounded C-stick up (past 53 of 80) presses X with the C-stick held back, then lets your C-stick through 1 frame later (never varied), inside the jumpsquat: a jump-cancelled up-smash, also out of a run. In a Fox/Falco shine, pushing or holding the C-stick up waits for the shine's Loop (the only shine state that takes a jump), then does the same; once per shine | Fixed gaps, so it works at any online delay. Like every ground mod, it does not start for jumpsquat + delay + 2 frames after a jump press (X, Y, a tap jump, or one the mods pressed): the state read online is a few frames old, and a C-stick up then is an up-air, an X a double jump, a Z a Z-air. X stays down while the C-stick does. In the air nothing changes (up-air) |
| Fox and Falco: B on the ground is always shine | `padmod_fox_falco_shine` | As Fox or Falco on the ground, your B goes in with the control stick at (0, -48), 0.60 down, on the same frame: down-B. The game takes down-B only on the frame B is pressed (`ftCo_800D688C`/`x687`) and only in states that check it then (Wait, walks, Turn, Run, RunDirect, Squat, SquatWait, SquatRv, teeter, Landing after its 4-frame lag), with no buffer; a B pressed earlier (end of a move, Dash, TurnRun, RunBrake, landing lag) is kept from the game and goes in once, with the stick, the moment such a state shows (up to 20 frames), then is held 2 frames | 0.60 is past the down-B line (PlCo `x21C` 0.55) and short of the platform drop line (`x464` 0.66): the shine's Start and Loop check a drop (`ftCo_80099F1C`), so a full-tilt stick from the mod dropped shines through platforms. One press only, never re-pressed (it would read as mashing). A held-back B that never gets taken, or any other button or C-stick push while it waits, drops it (never a late neutral-B). Left alone in jumpsquat, in the air and just after a jump press (lasers). Other characters are unchanged. Logged |
| Z on the ground always grabs | `padmod_z_always_grabs` | A grounded Z press always ends in a grab. The game grabs on a held shield input plus a fresh A, and Z is both; pressed a little early (landing lag, the end of a move) its A is spent and only a light shield is left. If the press did not grab, the shield is held with an analog press and Z is pressed again, up to 4 times, each judged `delay + 1` frames after it, so the grab (or shield grab) comes out as soon as you can act | Stops the moment a grab shows, so no pummel. In a Fox/Falco grounded shine, Z is a jump-cancelled grab instead: the Z is kept back until the shine is in its Loop (the only shine state that takes a jump), then X, and the Z one frame later (never varied), inside the jumpsquat. In the air Z is unchanged |
| Fox and Falco: hold L or R in a shine to wavedash | `padmod_shine_wavedash` | Holding L or R (pressed in the shine or already held) in a grounded shine: your L/R is kept from the game until the shine's Loop, then one X press, and your own L or R reaches the game on the first airborne frame (jumpsquat 3 frames for Fox, 5 for Falco; never varied, a frame later is an air dodge): an air dodge into the ground, a wavedash. Stick held sideways: that way, at your stick's angle when angled down past the deadzone (23), held flat the max-length wavedash on the stick's circle, (76, -24); with human timing 17.5-24 degrees down at random, never shallower (that is the deadzone). Stick in the middle: in place, an air dodge straight down, (0, -80); with human timing straight down +-4 degrees | In an air shine (Fox/Falco, 365-369), L or R is a double jump out of its Loop (`ftCo_800CB870`), then your L/R the very next frame: an air dodge, a waveland near the ground; needs the double jump left. Once per shine, so holding R through repeated B presses chains waveshines. If the jump does not come out (seen `delay + 1` frames after the X), L/R stay hidden until let go: no shield or roll. An analog-only press is sent as that side's click. Your own jump first (X or Y pressed in the shine, then L/R inside the jumpsquat, or L/R first and your jump while it waits): the mod presses no jump and sends your L/R on the first airborne frame counted from your jump press (11-08: replays showed Y-then-R waveshines landing a frame late or early). Logged |
| R on the ground: wavedash | `padmod_ground_wavedash` | Any fighter on the ground in a state that takes a jump (Wait through RunBrake: standing, walks, turn, dash, run; Squat through Landing; GuardOn, Guard): a fresh R (click, or analog past 80) is kept from the game, X goes down for 2 frames, and your R reaches the game on that fighter's first airborne frame, with a full press. Side and angle as the shine wavedash: the stick's side, your angle when angled down, else max length; stick in the middle, in place | Jumpsquat per fighter from each `Pl*.dat` (ftData attributes +0x38) of the NTSC 1.02 disc, e.g. Fox 3, Falcon 4, Falco 5, Bowser 8 (Zelda/Sheik use the character picked). R never shields on the ground (L does): the click and any analog R are kept from the game there; pressed where no jump is taken yet (landing lag, the end of a move) it waits up to 15 frames while held and goes as soon as one is; held on after the dodge it stays hidden through the landing. Your own jump first, then R inside the jumpsquat: R is kept back and sent on the first airborne frame counted from your jump press (no jump of the mod's, the jump's stick is yours); R on that frame or later is yours as it is; if your jump never came out, R is yours again. An R that starts in the air is left alone. Sub option **R alone shields (wavedash with your jump)** (`padmod_ground_wavedash_sub 1`, off by default): R shields again and only your own jump makes it a wavedash: jump then R, or R then jump (within 2 frames when the stick is pushed, 5 when R already went in as a shield, out of the shield incl. its GuardReflect start); an R with the stick pushed is kept back 2 frames for the jump, then goes in as pressed (shield, roll, spot dodge); R held on through the waveland stays hidden until let go. Lab 11-08 (Fox): R alone shield, R+Y right wavedash, shield then Y 3 frames later in-place wavedash, Y then R wavedash. Left alone in a Fox/Falco shine (that is the shine wavedash), and just after a jump press. If no jump shows `delay + 1` frames after the X, R stays hidden until let go. Logged. After the dodge (this and the shine wavedash), presses during the waveland lag (LandingFallSpecial, PlCo `x344` = 10 frames, no IASA, so Melee drops them) are held back: A, B, X, Y or a C-stick push goes in once on the first frame the fighter can act (released when the view shows the lag's 10th frame), aimed by the stick at that moment; Z and a Fox/Falco B are left to their own mods. Lab, Ganondorf: A in the lag = jab on the first free frame, C-up = up-smash. A trace of the next 50 frames' states is logged |
| Z while dashing or running: jump-cancelled grab | `padmod_run_jc_grab` | Z pressed in TurnRun, Dash, Run, RunDirect or RunBrake (19-23), any fighter: the jump goes in with Z (and A) kept back, then their Z one frame later (never varied), inside the jumpsquat, whose IASA checks the grab (`ftCo_KneeBend_IASA` -> `ftCo_Catch_CheckInput`): a standing grab carried by the run's momentum instead of a dash grab | Dash and Run check the grab (`ftCo_800D8A38`) before the jump (`fn_800CAF78`), so both on one frame would dash-grab; hence the 1-frame gap. Standing, walking, crouching, in the air and just after a jump press: Z unchanged. Takes the press from Z-always-grabs. Jump button per `padmod_jump_with_y` |

Their stick is theirs except around each B, where it eases into the laser zone keeping its direction (halfway, in, halfway back) | MEASURED headless 11-06 (`--match`, `@match` script, `--lcancel-log`, replay item events): B on the first airborne frame (3) starts the laser, laser 1 spawns at 12; a fresh B on 6..16 loops it, laser 2 spawns at 22 = the landing frame, so B 1 can be no later. IN THE AIR (the first laser, a full hop, a drop off a platform): a laser loop just (re)started gets a B (the refire reads only B, not the stick: `ftFox_SpecialN_CheckLoopInput`), so the first laser gets its second one and Fox keeps firing; jumping or falling with no laser starts a new one. Lab: the manual first hop fires 2 lasers, a full hop fires all the way down. While waiting out the landing lag the held B stays hidden (it would reach the game as a fresh press: a ground laser) |

| Auto L-cancel (ours, also in Teams) | `padmod_auto_lcancel` | The same trigger press as the game's own Auto L-cancel setting (`lcancel.cpp`), under its own switch: offline, Direct and Teams | The game's Auto L-cancel setting keeps its stock modes (offline and Direct).
